# Joud brand sheet for Joud Bird VR

Source: "JOUD CAMPAIGN 2026" print file by TONE design studio (local copy:
`docs/brand/JOUD_26_PRINT_FILE.pdf`, page images in `docs/brand/pages/`, never committed).
Joud has granted permission and DSC holds the font licences. This sheet describes the brand;
it does not reproduce Joud's artwork.

## Character and story
- Official Arabic name: **العصفورة جود** (short form: عصفورة جود). Use this, not "طائر جود".
- Story (page 4): the bird knows one thing: every good deed is a seed, every seed becomes a fruit,
  and the real fruit is the impact people leave. She visits cafés and restaurants and sits with
  you through every experience: your cup, your plate, a conversation.
- Hashtag: **#طلعتنا_اليوم**

are used sparingly ("!!" only on the bold poster).

|---|---|---|
| Arabic display (all campaign headlines and taglines) | **DG Mataryah** | Heavy, blocky, geometric; uses long kashida stretches ("جـــود", "ادّعـــم") as a graphic device |
| Latin labels (TONE's document layout only) | Atyp Display | The agency's presentation font, **not** part of the Joud campaign look; don't use it in the game |

The PDF only embeds tiny subsets (DG Mataryah ≈ 8 KB, a dozen glyphs, no Arabic shaping tables),
so the licensed font files must come from DSC's licence (see `docs/brand/fonts/README.md`).

## Visual motifs
- Hand-painted gouache florals: big red, pink and blue flowers with orange centres, dark green
  leaves and stems, small sparkles (four-point stars) and crescent moons.
- Flat, saturated colour blocks; no gradients in the artwork, no gloss.
- Stacked boxes (100 × 50 cm campaign boxes) with one word each: أنا / ادّعم / جود.
- Cafés: cups and cup sleeves with the bird peeking over the rim.
- The bird appears whole and friendly; silhouettes and peeking poses are used on posters.

## Game application
- Obstacles: stacked campaign boxes in yellow / green / red-orange, with painted-flower faces.
- World: butter-yellow or soft blue sky, a ground strip of painted flowers and leaves.
- Score effects: petals and four-point sparkles in the campaign colours.
- Panels: solid colour blocks (green or red-orange) with cream DG Mataryah headlines; the
  bird peeking from a panel corner.
- Keep text Arabic-first, right-aligned, large.
